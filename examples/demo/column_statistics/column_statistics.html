Column statistics demo

Output of ord_schema.analysis.columns: one chart for each group of columns a dataset varies. Numbers with many distinct values get a histogram; anything else gets the count of each value, every column of a group side by side. Hover over a bar for its full value and count. Provenance groups have a dashed border.
